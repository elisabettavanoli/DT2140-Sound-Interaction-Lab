    • Interaction 3 — Shake → Bell

    Action: the phone is shaken.
    Sound: bell.

    Scenario: a hand bell, like a school or reception bell, which you ring by shaking it.
    Mapping: each shake gesture is one strike, a discrete event, so it triggers a single bell stroke (with a short cooldown, so one gesture does not cause a burst of strokes). The strength of the shake controls the stroke: a harder shake rings louder and brighter, like hitting a real bell harder. Small movements do not ring the bell.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP" and shake the phone. Adjust the sensitivity with the "shake threshold" slider.
