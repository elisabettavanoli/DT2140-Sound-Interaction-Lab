    • Interaction 3 — Shake → Bells

    Action: the phone is shaken.
    Sound: bells.

    Scenario: a festive bell peal, like a bell tower ringing on a holiday.
    Mapping: a single shake is one strike, so it rings one bell. Shaking continuously rings more and more bells, faster and overlapping, so the energy and duration of the gesture become the density of the sound. Each strike picks one of four different bells (English, French, Russian, church), with its own pitch and timbre. A harder shake rings louder and brighter, like hitting a real bell harder. Small movements do not ring the bells.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP" and shake the phone once, then keep shaking. Adjust the sensitivity with the "shake threshold" slider.
