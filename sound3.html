    • Interaction 3 — Shake → Bells

    Action: the phone is shaken.
    Sound: bells.

    Scenario: a festive bell peal, like a bell tower ringing on a holiday.
    Mapping: one shake is like striking a bell once: a single, discrete gesture gives a single, discrete sound. Shaking harder strikes harder, so the bell rings louder and brighter. Shaking continuously rings four different bells (English, French, Russian and church bell, each with its own pitch and timbre) faster and faster, so the strokes overlap and ring together: the energy and duration of the gesture become the density of the sound. Small movements do not ring the bells.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP" and shake the phone once, then keep shaking. Adjust the sensitivity with the "shake threshold" slider.
