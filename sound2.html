    • Interaction 2 — Large movements → Wind

    Action: the phone is held and used to detect all very large movements, but not small ones.
    Sound: wind.

    Scenario: the gust of wind that lifts a kite, or the "whoosh" of a stick swung through the air.
    Mapping: only a large, fast movement pushes enough air to be heard, so small movements stay silent: below a threshold the movement is ignored. Above it, the bigger the sweep, the stronger and louder the gust. The gust builds up with the gesture and fades out slowly after it, like moving air that settles.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP", hold the phone and sweep your arm through the air. Small movements stay silent.
