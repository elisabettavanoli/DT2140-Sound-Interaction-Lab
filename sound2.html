    • Interaction 2 — Large movements → Wind

    Action: the phone is held and used to detect all very large movements, but not small ones.
    Sound: wind.

    Scenario: swinging a stick, a fan or a flag through the air.
    Mapping: a big, fast sweep pushes air and makes a "whoosh", while small movements don't move enough air to be heard. Movements below a threshold are ignored (silence); above it, the strength of the movement controls the force and volume of the gust. The gust builds up quickly with the gesture and fades out slowly after it, like air that keeps moving for a moment.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP", hold the phone and sweep your arm through the air. Small movements stay silent.
