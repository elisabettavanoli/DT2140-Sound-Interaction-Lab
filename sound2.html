    • Interaction 2 — Spin → Engine

    Action: the phone is spun while lying on a flat surface.
    Sound: engine.

    Scenario: a spinning top or crank that drives a motor.
    Mapping: a motor is something that turns, so the phone's rotation speed controls the engine's revs. The engine starts only when the phone lies flat and spins fast enough. Spinning faster makes the revs higher, and when the phone slows down the engine winds down and stops. The 360°→0° jump of the compass angle is unwrapped, and separate start/stop thresholds keep the engine from flickering on and off.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP", lay the phone on a table and spin it.
