    • Interaction 1 — Tilt → Rain

    Action: the phone is placed flat in the hand and tilted from side to side.
    Sound: rain.

    Scenario: a digital rain stick. A real rain stick is played by tilting it: the seeds inside slide down and sound like rain.
    Mapping: the tilt angle (to either side) controls the rain density and volume. Flat = silence, slightly tilted = a light drizzle, about 60° = heavy rain. A continuous gesture controls a continuous sound, and more tilt means more "falling seeds", so the response is easy to predict.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP", hold the phone flat and tilt it left or right.
