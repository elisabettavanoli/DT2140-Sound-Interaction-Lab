    • Interaction 1 — Tilt → Rain

    Action: the phone is placed flat in the hand and tilted from side to side.
    Sound: rain.

    Scenario: tilting a bowl of water: the more you tilt it, the more water pours out.
    Mapping: in the same way, the tilt angle (to either side) controls the rain density and volume. Held flat, nothing falls (silence); a small tilt gives a drizzle and a strong tilt (about 60°) a downpour. A continuous gesture controls a continuous sound, so you can "pour" exactly the amount of rain you want.

    How to use: press "Turn On DSP", hold the phone flat and tilt it left or right.
